Quick Guide

How to use RabbitCalc

  1. Tap the breeding date. RabbitCalc builds the schedule automatically.
  2. Follow the marked dates for palpation, nest box, expected kindle, eyes opening, and weaning.
  3. Use 1M / 3M after selecting a date to switch between focused and full-cycle views.
  4. Tap a marked date to see what that milestone means.
  5. Clear Breeding Date to start a new calculation.
Rabbit dates naturally vary. The calendar is a planning aid, not a guarantee of an exact biological date.
